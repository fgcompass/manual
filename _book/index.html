Preface

It is worth noting that while users may videotape performances for later assessment, the FG-COMPASS is primarily designed for live, in situ assessment. This unique feature allows practitioners to observe and assess children’s FMS proficiency in real-time without needing performance recordings, enhancing the assessment’s convenience.

VI. Psychometric Properties

  • Reliability:Inter-rater reliability: The degree of agreement between different raters assessing the same performance. [27]

  • High inter-rater reliability has been demonstrated in previous studies, particularly for manipulative skills. [6, 28, 29]

  • Intra-rater reliability: The consistency of a single rater’s scores over time. [27]

  • Good intra-rater reliability has been demonstrated for both locomotor and manipulative skills. [6, 28, 29]

  • Validity:Concurrent validity: The extent to which the FG-COMPASS scores correlate with scores from an established assessment tool (TGMD-2). [6]

  • Good to excellent concurrent validity has been demonstrated for the FG-COMPASS, particularly for manipulative skills and the total test score. [6, 29]

  • Expert-rater agreement: High levels of agreement between expert raters and trained non-expert raters have been established, supporting the accuracy of the rating scales. [30-33]

VII. Future Directions and Limitations

  • Future Research:Adding two more skills to the locomotor subtest to enhance its comprehensiveness [5, 28]

  • Continued investigation of variations in rater interpretations, particularly for locomotor skills [6, 28]

  • Evaluating the feasibility and effectiveness of the web-based training tool and the mobile version of the instrument in real-world settings [26, 28]

  • Investigating the impact of “bad trials” during live assessments and developing strategies for managing them [28]

  • Limitations:The locomotor subtest requires further development with the addition of two more skills. [5, 28]

  • Potential variations in rater interpretations, especially for locomotor skills, need to be addressed through training and scoring criteria refinement. [6, 28]

  • The effectiveness of the web-based training and mobile version needs to be empirically evaluated. [26, 28]
